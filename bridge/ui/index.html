Lens

Each lens has its own end-stop direction, slip reference and distance map. After changing lenses: mount the motor firmly, select the lens, Calibrate.

Calibration

Drives the lens to both end stops (~10–15 s), sets 0 % = closest focus and checks the gear for slip. Needed after every motor power-on and every lens change.
